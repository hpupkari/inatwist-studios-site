How a title is made

Four stages, and a
person at the end.

  • 1

    Adapted as scenes

    Each book is rebuilt as scenes and cut into episodes of thirty to forty minutes, each opening with a recap so a week away costs nothing.

  • 2

    Cast, part by part

    Every character's voice is designed from a written description, auditioned three ways and chosen. Never cloned from a real person.

  • 3

    Scored and placed

    Named themes that belong to a character and recur, not library cues. Weather, rooms and distance built around the performance.

  • 4

    Heard before release

    Measured for level and clarity, then listened to end to end and approved by a person.

The cast is performed by synthetic voices designed for each part, never cloned from a real person, and AI writing tools are used in drafting. Every episode is listened to end to end and approved by a person before release.
